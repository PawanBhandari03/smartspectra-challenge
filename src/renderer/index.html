Signal Lab

For the decisions you keep going back and forth on

Your body votes on your decisions.

What are you torn between? Picture living each option while your camera quietly reads your pulse, breathing and blinks. Then see where your body reacted, moment by moment.

A reflection tool for general wellness, not medical advice. Video never leaves your computer.

How a check works · about four minutes

  1. i.
    Name two optionsThe choices you're torn between, in your own words.
  2. ii.
    Imagine each, twiceShort guided scenes in a mirrored order, about four minutes.
  3. iii.
    Read your mirrorWhat your body did, honestly reported, including "no clear side".

What are you deciding between?

Name your two options and read the terms beside them. Keep each option short; the optional detail makes the scenes more vivid.

Before we start

  • 1
    Your camera measures, nothing is filmed. Video is processed on this computer. Only numbers are saved, on this computer.
  • 2
    It's a mirror, not a decision machine. A racing heart can mean fear or excitement. The result is a prompt to reflect, never a verdict.
  • 3
    Not medical advice. CrossRoads is a general wellness tool and doesn't diagnose anything.
  • 4
    You can stop at any time.

Camera check

Camera is off

  • Light on your face from the front, nothing bright behind you
  • Face inside the oval, upper chest visible
  • Camera completely still (on a stand or the desk)
  • Sit back with your back supported; it takes about 4 minutes
  • Read the prompts silently; stay still while you imagine
