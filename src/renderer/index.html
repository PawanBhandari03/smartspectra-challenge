Gut Check

Your body votes on your decisions.

Stuck between options? Imagine living each one while your webcam quietly reads your pulse, breathing, blinks and expressions. Then see which option your body relaxed into, and which one it tensed up at.

  1. Name your two options the choices you're torn between
  2. Imagine each one, twice short guided scenes, one minute per visit
  3. See your Body Vote only when both rounds agree; otherwise it honestly says "too close to call"

A reflection tool for general wellness, not medical advice. Video never leaves your computer.

Step 2 of 3

Before we start

  • Your camera measures, nothing is filmed. Video is processed on this computer. Only numbers (pulse, breathing, blinks, expressions) are saved, on this computer.
  • It's a mirror, not a decision machine. A racing heart can mean fear or excitement. The result is a prompt to reflect, never a verdict.
  • Not medical advice. Gut Check is a general wellness tool and doesn't diagnose anything.
  • You can stop at any time.
