Step 1 of 3

What are you deciding between?

Keep each option short. The optional detail makes the scenes more vivid.

Step 2 of 3

Before we start

  • 1
    Your camera measures, nothing is filmed. Video is processed on this computer. Only numbers are saved, on this computer.
  • 2
    It's a mirror, not a decision machine. A racing heart can mean fear or excitement. The result is a prompt to reflect, never a verdict.
  • 3
    Not medical advice. Gut Check is a general wellness tool and doesn't diagnose anything.
  • 4
    You can stop at any time.

Step 3 of 3

Camera check

Camera is off

  • Light on your face from the front, nothing bright behind you
  • Face inside the oval, upper chest visible
  • Camera completely still (on a stand or the desk)
  • Sit back with your back supported; it takes about 4 minutes
  • Read the prompts silently; stay still while you imagine
